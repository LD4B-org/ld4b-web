Supporting info

Laser coupling technology

Our business focuses on optical fiber alignment. We specialize in fiber-coupling and packaging laser diodes (LD) and superluminescent diodes (SLD) in a TO-can.

We focus on the most challenging fiber coupling to single-mode (SM) and polarization-maintaining (PM) optical fibers with small core diameters. The beam at the output from FC/UPC or FC/APC connector is close to Gaussian that allows for high-quality beam collimation. The modules are secured in compact hermetically sealed packages.

Our laser diode and SLD modules undergo thorough control at every stage of production. We prioritize cleanliness and electrostatic discharge (ESD) protection. The design of these fiber-coupled modules is rigorously tested in accordance with Telcordia GR-468-CORE standards to ensure reliability and performance.

Our fiber-coupled laser diode and SLD modules deliver high coupling efficiency with excellent thermal stability. For the modules without active thermal stabilization the performance is characterized by the front-to-rear tracking error (ER) = 10 log[P(T)/P(25°C)], where the temperature varies within a specific range (e.g., from -40°C to +85°C), while the monitor photodiode signal remains constant.

Through meticulous module design and optimized coupling optics, we achieve a typical tracking error (ER) of 0.3 dB (representing a coupling efficiency variation within ±7%) for telecom wavelengths over the temperature range of -40°C to +85°C. Standard laser diode modules, by comparison, can show a tracking error of up to 1 dB (±20%).

We also offer modules with active thermal stabilization with a thermoelectric cooler (TEC) and thermistor in 14-pin BTF packages and in the compact coaxial COAXB package (the coaxTEC series).

Explore detailed specifications and performance characteristics in our datasheets to find the optimal laser diode module or superluminescent diode module for your needs or contact us for customized solutions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is special about LD4B’s laser diode pigtailing technology?

LD4B employs two specialized pigtailing technologies tailored for different application priorities. The first technology achieves higher coupling efficiency of 70–80 % with a typical thermal variation of about 0.5 dB in the telecom wavelength range. The second technology prioritizes exceptional stability of the coupling against temperature fluctuations, with a tracking error of 0.2–0.3 dB at the cost of lower coupling efficiency of 50–60 %. These options enable customers to choose fiber coupling solutions optimized either for maximum optical power or for superior thermal stability, depending on their system requirements.

How does LD4B test the reliability of pigtailed modules?

LD4B focuses on the reliability of fiber alignment and packaging in our assemblies. We rigorously test our module designs following the Telcordia GR-468-CORE standard, a leading industry benchmark for optical component reliability. Our comprehensive testing includes mechanical shock and vibration, multiple thermal cycling, high and low temperature storage, thermal shocks, fiber integrity, and leak tests. During production every module passes 10 passive thermal cycles from −40 °C to +85 °C. These tests ensure our modules deliver stable, long-term performance in demanding industrial environments.

Under what conditions does LD4B assemble pigtailed modules?

LD4B assembles pigtailed modules in controlled clean environment within laminar flow hoods to maintain high standards of cleanliness and contamination control. Strict precautions against electrostatic discharge (ESD) are implemented throughout the process. Each assembled module undergoes thorough electrical and optical testing to ensure optimal performance before delivery.
