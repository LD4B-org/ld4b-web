Supporting info

Applications

Where LD4B fiber-coupled modules are used, with the matching products.

Gas sensing

Tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy needs a single-frequency laser held at the absorption line of the gas. Our DFB lasers in DIL and butterfly packages with TEC and thermistor keep the wavelength where the line is.

The 1653.7 nm DFB laser targets the methane absorption line used in leak detection and emission monitoring.

Distributed sensing and OTDR

Fiber-optic perimeter intrusion detection, distributed temperature and strain sensing, and OTDR rely on stable pulsed sources and sensitive receivers.

We offer DFB lasers with instantaneous linewidth below 500 kHz, DFB lasers with 50 mW in single-mode fiber, 1625 nm and 1650 nm lasers for in-service fiber monitoring, and avalanche photodiodes with low back-reflection.

Microwave photonics

Analog fiber links and radio over fiber need linear lasers and photodiodes that stay linear at high optical power.

Our PIN photodiodes accept up to 50 mW of optical input and reach bandwidths up to 20 GHz; our DFB lasers are available for 2.5 Gbps and 10 Gbps modulation.

OCT, gyroscopes and fiber sensors

Superluminescent diodes are broadband, low-coherence sources for optical coherence tomography, fiber-optic gyroscopes and fiber sensors.

Our SLDs cover 830, 980, 1310 and 1550 nm, coupled to single-mode or polarization-maintaining fiber, in coaxial packages or with TEC in DIL and butterfly packages.

SLDs and LEDs

Telecom, PON and test

Test sources and transmitters need the right channel wavelength in a stable package.

We offer DFB lasers across the CWDM grid from 1270 nm to 1610 nm, 1577 nm for PON, and PIN-TIA and APD-TIA receivers from 2.5 to 25 Gbps.
