Applications

Where LD4B fiber-coupled modules are used, with matching products for each application.

OTDR and distributed sensing

Fiber-optic perimeter intrusion detection, distributed temperature and strain sensing, and OTDR rely on stable pulsed sources and sensitive receivers.

We offer Fabry-Perot and DFB laser diodes as well as sensitive PIN and avalanche photodiodes.

Microwave photonics

Analog fiber links and radio-over-fiber systems need lasers and photodiodes that stay linear at high optical power.

We offer PIN photodiodes in coaxial packages with 10 GHz and 20 GHz bandwidth that handle tens of milliwatts of optical input, and DFB laser diodes with modulation bandwidth up to 10 GHz.

PhotodiodesLaser diodes Telecom

Gas sensing

Tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy (TDLAS) needs a single-frequency laser held at the absorption line of the gas.

Our DFB lasers in packages with a TEC and a thermistor keep the wavelength on the absorption line.

Laser diodes Telecom

OCT and fiber sensors

Superluminescent diodes (SLDs) are broadband, low-coherence light sources for optical coherence tomography (OCT) and fiber sensors.

Our SLDs cover 830, 980, 1310 and 1550 nm and are coupled to single-mode or polarization-maintaining fiber, in coaxial packages or in butterfly packages with a TEC.

SLDs and LEDs

Free-space optical (FSO) communication

We offer 2.5 Gbps and 10 Gbps APD-TIA and PIN-TIA receivers pigtailed with multimode fiber, which makes them easy to couple to a telescope in FSO links.

Photodiodes

Laboratory and research

A wide range of scientific and research applications require fiber-coupled light sources and detectors. We offer pigtailed modules as components as well as simple-to-use plug-and-play systems with controllers.

The TH package fits the popular Thorlabs LDM9LP mount and CLD101xLP controller.

The compact laser module with a manual controller runs any LD4B laser diode from a USB-C power source.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where are LD4B products used?

LD4B products are widely used in microwave photonics, optical time-domain reflectometry, telecommunications, datacom, fiber sensing, medical equipment, and scientific research.

How do I choose the right LD4B light source or photodetector for my application?

Start with the wavelength, spectrum, power and speed your application needs. Choose a DFB laser for a single-frequency, narrow-linewidth source (telecom, CWDM, sensing, spectroscopy), an FP laser when you simply need power at a given wavelength (500 to 1650 nm, up to 100 mW from the fiber), an SLD for broadband, low-coherence light (OCT, fiber sensing), and a TEC-cooled version when wavelength stability matters. For detection, a PIN photodiode covers monitoring and high-speed analog links up to 20 GHz, an APD gives extra sensitivity for weak signals such as in LIDAR and OTDR, and our PIN-TIA and APD-TIA receivers are ready-made solutions for digital links up to 25 Gbps. Then pick the fiber (SM, PM or MM), the connector and the package: every module is built to your configuration. If you're not sure, send your requirements to contact@ld4b.com and we'll recommend the right part.

What technical information should I send to LD4B to get a product recommendation or a quote?

Please send your application, wavelength, optical power, modulation speed, operating temperature, fiber type, connector, package and quantity. If you're unsure about any of these, just describe your application and we'll help you choose.
