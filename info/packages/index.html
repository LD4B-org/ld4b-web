Supporting info

Packages

We offer pigtailed laser diodes, SLDs and photodiodes in various packages without and with thermal stabilization.

COAX

No thermal stabilization

The most compact and lightweight coaxial package for the modules with low heat release (for example, laser diodes in low duty cycle pulse mode or photodiodes).

PDF Application note: package COAX 80 KB

COAXB

No thermal stabilization

The package COAXB is the same compact coaxial package with a high thermal conductance bracket for convenient mounting to a heat sink. Remember to use thermal grease.

PDF Application note: package COAXB 137 KB

COAXH

No thermal stabilization

The package COAXH is the same compact coaxial package with an H-shaped bracket for convenient mounting to a board. Remember to use thermal grease.

PDF Application note: package COAXH 77 KB

TH

No thermal stabilization

The package TH is also the coaxial package with a bracket that fits popular Thorlabs' LDM9LP mount and CLD101xLP controller.

PDF Application note: package TH 78 KB

BTF

TEC and thermistor

A BTF ("Butterfly") package is a popular design of housing for diodes. It includes a TEC and a thermistor for thermal stabilization. Its compact, heat-dissipating design ensures precise alignment, electrical connections, and protection from contaminants in various applications.

FAQ

Questions about packages

Which package should I choose?

Choose by heat load and mounting. COAX is the most compact and lightweight package and can be soldered directly into a PCB; without a heat sink it suits photodiodes, laser diodes in pulse mode with a small duty cycle, or CW operation at a small current, as long as the total released heat stays below 30 mW. COAXB and COAXH add a bracket for mounting to a heat sink, a device case or a board, and suit CW laser diodes and SLDs. TH fits the Thorlabs LDM9LP mount and CLD101xLP controller. BTF has a TEC and a thermistor and is the right choice when the wavelength and optical power must stay stable.

Does my module need a heat sink?

For long and reliable operation, every module should be attached to a heat sink, unless it is a COAX module releasing less than 30 mW of heat. The COAXB bracket has M2 threaded holes on the top and bottom sides and holes without thread or with M2.5 thread on the front side for panel mounting. A BTF module always needs a heat sink, because both the laser diode and the TEC release heat. Remember to use thermal grease.

What are the thermistor and TEC parameters of the BTF package?

The thermistor is an NTC thermistor with R0 = 10 kOhm at 25 °C (T0 = 298 K) and B = 3600 K, so the absolute temperature follows from 1/T = 1/T0 + (1/B) ln(R/R0). We offer two TEC types: LP for laser diodes releasing up to 250–300 mW of heat (Imax = 0.7 A, Umax = 3.9 V, Qmax = 1.4 W, AC resistance 4.7 Ohm, dTmax = 72 K) and HP for larger heat loads (Imax = 1.4 A, Umax = 3.9 V, Qmax = 3.3 W, AC resistance 2.0 Ohm, dTmax = 69 K). The BTF application note shows the resistance curve and the measured cooling performance.
