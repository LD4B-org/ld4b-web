19 October 2026

Two photonics events in Poland in October

Poland is a good place to do photonics. In October alone, there were two events worth the trip.

On 19 October, Andrei Andryieuski attended the 11th Workshop on Physics and Technology of Semiconductor Lasers in Toruń, organized by the Candela Foundation and Lodz University of Technology. The talks he found most interesting:

  • GaN DFB laser diodes: Scott Watson (University of Glasgow) and Saad Makhladi (Ferdinand-Braun-Institut, Leibniz-Institut für Höchstfrequenztechnik)
  • Degradation mechanisms in nitride laser diodes: Piotr Perlin (Institute of High Pressure Physics PAS)
  • Ultraviolet photonic-crystal surface-emitting lasers: Åsa Haglund (Chalmers University of Technology)
  • Quantum cascade lasers for free-space optical communication: Michał Nagowski (Łukasiewicz – IMIF)

On 28 October, Andrei also attended the Swiss-Polish Photonics Business Evening in Ożarów Mazowiecki, organized by VIGO Photonics, Swissmem, SEP (Polish Committee of Optoelectronics) and PPTF (Polish Technological Platform on Photonics). It was an evening of good discussions with photonics companies from both countries.

Thank you to the organizers and speakers. If we met at either event and you would like to continue the conversation, feel free to reach out.

7–8 October 2026

Photonics Days Berlin Brandenburg

Our CEO, Andrei Andryieuski, attended Photonics Days Berlin Brandenburg on 7–8 October at Technologiepark Adlershof. The event brings institutes and companies, researchers and business people together.

His main takeaway: many ideas that were mostly discussed in research labs 20 years ago, like hybrid photonic integration and photonic interconnects, are now being put into practice in industry. The gap between a theoretical concept and a working product keeps getting shorter.

Among the talks, Andrei highlighted Dr. Adrian Mahlkow's (OpTecBB) overview of current trends in the photonics industry, and David de Felipe Mesquida's presentation of the activities and results at Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute HHI.

Prof. Dr. Ulrich H. P. Fischer (Hochschule Harz) gave an overview of coupling methods between dielectric photonic waveguides, a topic close to Andrei's own PhD work. Andrei recalls: “Back in 2011 I thought this area was largely exhausted, and that it would be difficult to invent anything new. I was surprised to see new approaches like 3D-printed photonic wire bonding, and older technologies being improved to meet industrial requirements for temperature stability and alignment tolerance.”

These are the same parameters our team works with every day when fiber-coupling laser diodes in Warsaw.

Andrei also had an interesting technical discussion with Dr. Oliver Lischtschenko of Coher Sense, who build compact interferometric wavelength and power meters around a smart device design.

If you were at the event and would like to continue the conversation, feel free to reach out.

21 September 2026

LD4B at ECOC 2026 in Malaga

Our CEO Andrei Andryieuski spent a day at the ECOC Exhibition 2026 in Malaga, packed with meetings and intensive discussions.

What stood out most was the progress in optical interconnects, driven by the growing demands of AI infrastructure and data centers. The field is clearly accelerating.

ECOC is also where old connections come back together. Andrei was glad to reconnect with colleagues from his PhD and postdoc years at the Technical University of Denmark, who now do impressive work across the industry:

  • Dagmawi Alemayehu Bekele, building sustainable high-speed interconnects at Phanofi
  • Peter Moselund, leading photonic integration at the Swiss Photonics Integration Center (Swiss PIC)

The photonics community stays close. It was good to see familiar faces pushing things forward.
