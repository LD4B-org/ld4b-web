News

BTF package

Last year, a recurring question from our valued customers sparked an advancement: "Why not offer laser diodes in the industry-standard butterfly case?"

Your feedback is our command! We are glad to announce that we now offer fiber-coupled laser diodes housed in the sleek, efficient 14-pin Butterfly case, featuring pinout type 1 (pump). This Butterfly case ensures durability, superior heat management, and seamless integration into your existing systems.

New SLDs 830 and 980 nm

We are glad to present our newly introduced fiber-coupled SLDs with mean wavelengths of 830 nm and 980 nm, delivering an optical power of 2 mW out of the fiber. These pigtailed modules in coaxial case are cost-efficient solutions for the applications where thermal stabilization is not required.

LD4B in EPIC

We're thrilled to announce our latest step forward: becoming a part of the vibrant community EPIC - EUROPEAN PHOTONICS INDUSTRY CONSORTIUM.

We're excited about the prospects of forging new collaborations, learning from industry leaders, and contributing our own innovations to this dynamic field.
