About us

Who we are

LD4B was founded in Warsaw, Poland, in 2022. We are a team of engineers and scientists with a passion for photonics, and our experience in fiber-coupling laser diodes goes back many years before the company was founded.

Our mission is to align optical fibers efficiently and to manufacture reliable fiber-coupled laser diodes, SLDs and photodiodes. Our modules are used in equipment for telecommunications, sensing, microwave photonics, life sciences and scientific research.

Our facility in Warsaw

Design and manufacturing take place under one roof at our facility in Warsaw. We design the modules and their packages, couple the diodes to the fiber, assemble the modules and check them at every stage of production, in a clean environment with ESD protection.

This is why you talk directly to the engineers who develop and build your module: technical questions are answered by the people who know the design.

How we work

Our modules come in many configurations: you choose the package type, fiber type, fiber length and connector. We therefore do not keep ready modules in stock, but manufacture each one as soon as possible after the order is placed.

There is no minimum order quantity for the modules on our website: we accept orders from 1 pc.

We are open to collaboration and ready to adapt the design to your needs.

How to order

Contact us by email or through the form below, and describe the technical parameters of the module you need or give the exact part number from the datasheet. We will get back to you as soon as possible to discuss the details and to offer the price and lead time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does LD4B mean?

LD4B stands for “Laser Diode for Business.” The name reflects our focus on providing high-quality optoelectronic solutions. While our roots are in laser diodes, we also manufacture superluminescent diodes (SLDs) and photodiodes, all with advanced fiber-coupling technology.

Who works at LD4B?

LD4B is driven by a team of engineers and scientists passionate about photonics. Our expertise covers precise optical fiber alignment and the manufacturing of reliable fiber-coupled laser diodes, superluminescent diodes (SLDs), and photodiodes.

Is LD4B a manufacturer?

Yes. LD4B manufactures fiber-coupled modules, but we do not grow wafers or make semiconductor chips. We source laser diodes, SLDs and photodiodes in TO-cans from chip manufacturers and specialize in what happens next: fiber coupling, active alignment, packaging and testing, carried out at our facility in Warsaw, Poland.
