APDTIA4B-10G-W 1250–1650 nm fiber-coupled avalanche photodiode with TIA, 10 Gbps

Avalanche photodiode with a low-noise transimpedance amplifier with auto gain control coupled to an optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Fiber

Main features

  • High-sensitivity avalanche photodiode with limiting TIA
  • Operation wavelength 1250 – 1650 nm
  • Data rate: 10 Gbps
  • Sensitivity: -29 dBm
  • Package types: coaxial with or without bracket
  • Low back reflection, return loss RL = 45 dB
