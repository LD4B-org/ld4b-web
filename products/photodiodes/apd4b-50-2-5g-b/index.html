APD4B-50-2.5G-B 850–1650 nm fiber-coupled avalanche photodiode, 2.5 GHz

Avalanche photodiode coupled to an optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Main features

  • Bandwidth: 2.5 GHz
  • Spectral range: 850 - 1650 nm
  • Typical responsivity 1 A/W at 1550 nm and M = 1
  • Package types: coaxial with or without bracket
  • Low dark current typ. 1 nA at 0.9 Vbr
