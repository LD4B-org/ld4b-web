APD4B-200-0.7G-A

Avalanche photodiode coupled to a multimode optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Request a quote Datasheet PDF (561 KB)

Main features

  • Spectral range: 900 - 1650 nm
  • Package types: coaxial with or without bracket
  • Low dark current typ. 30 nA at 0.9 Vbr
  • Can be operated at gain > 120
  • Low temperature coefficient of breakdown voltage 0.02 V/C
  • Fast overload recovery at high gain
