Photodiodes

Fiber-coupled PIN photodiodes

13 fiber-coupled PIN photodiodes: 400 to 2150 nm, bandwidth 1 to 20 GHz, input power up to 50 mW. Packages COAX, COAXB and BTF, with SMF and MMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

PIN photodiodes convert light into current with high responsivity, low noise and a large linear range. Our range covers a silicon diode for 400 to 1000 nm, InGaAs diodes for 500 or 800 to 1700 nm, and an extended-InGaAs diode up to 2150 nm.

Typical applications of our PIN photodiodes include microwave photonics and RF over fiber, where our high-power parts take tens of milliwatts of input at 10 and 20 GHz bandwidth; analog optical links; optical power monitoring and power meters; fiber-optic perimeter intrusion detection systems (FOPIDS); fiber sensing systems; measurements in the 2 µm band with the extended-InGaAs diode; characterization of lasers and modulators, such as bandwidth and noise; and research and education.

Tell us about your application, and we will help you choose the active area, bandwidth, coupling and fiber.
