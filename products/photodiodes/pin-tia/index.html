Fiber-coupled PIN photodiodes with TIA

4 fiber-coupled PIN receivers with TIA: 770 to 1620 nm, 2.5, 4.25, 10 and 25 Gbps. Packages COAX and COAXB, with SMF and MMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

PIN-TIA modules combine a PIN photodiode with a transimpedance amplifier with automatic gain control, giving a ready receiver for digital links. They run from 3.3 V.

Typical applications of our PIN-TIA receivers include digital optical links from 2.5 to 25 Gbps at 1260 to 1620 nm; short links at 850 nm over multimode fiber with the GaAs receiver, paired with a VCSEL; free-space optical (FSO) communication with a multimode fiber pigtail; testing and evaluation of optical transmitters; and research and education.

Tell us about your application, and we will help you choose the data rate, package and fiber.
