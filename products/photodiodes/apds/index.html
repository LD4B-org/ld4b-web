Photodiodes

Fiber-coupled avalanche photodiodes

6 fiber-coupled avalanche photodiodes (APDs): 800 to 1650 nm, bandwidth 0.7 to 3.5 GHz. Packages COAX, COAXB and COAXH, with SMF and MMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

Avalanche photodiodes (APDs) multiply the photocurrent inside the chip, so they detect much weaker light than PIN photodiodes. They need a high reverse bias, 47 to 65 V for our InGaAs APDs, compensated for temperature.

Typical applications of our APDs include OTDR and fiber monitoring; development and testing of LiDAR and range finders; measurements at low light levels; free-space optical (FSO) communication, where a multimode fiber pigtail makes the module easy to couple to a telescope; and research and education.

Tell us about your application, and we will help you choose the active area, bandwidth, coupling and fiber. For a ready receiver with an amplifier, see our APD-TIA modules.
