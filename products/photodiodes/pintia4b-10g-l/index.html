1260–1620 nm fiber-coupled PIN photodiode with TIA, 10 Gbps

PINTIA4B-10G-L

PIN photodiode with a low-noise transimpedance amplifier with auto gain control coupled to an optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Fiber

Main features

  • Maximum optical input power 1.1 mW
  • Operation wavelength 1260 – 1620 nm
  • Data rate: 10 Gbps
  • Sensitivity: -15.5 dBm
  • Package types: coaxial with or without bracket
  • Low back reflection, return loss RL = 50 dB
