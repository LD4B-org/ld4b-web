Laser diodes Telecom

We offer a diverse selection of single-mode (SM), polarization-maintaining (PM), and multimode (MM) optical fiber-coupled laser diodes, also known as pigtailed laser diodes. Our range includes Fabry-Perot, distributed-feedback (DFB), and vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser (VCSEL) diodes, covering wavelengths from 500 nm to 1653 nm, available in various packages to suit different applications. These semiconductor laser diodes are designed with precision to provide coherent laser light, making them ideal for optoelectronic applications such as microwave photonics, OTDR, fiber-optic perimeter intrusion detection systems, optical communication, spectroscopy, sensing, and other photonic systems.
