Laser diodes Telecom

We offer a diverse selection of laser diodes coupled to single-mode (SMF), polarization-maintaining (PMF) and multimode (MMF) optical fibers, also known as pigtailed laser diodes. Our range includes Fabry-Perot, distributed-feedback (DFB), and vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser (VCSEL) diodes, covering wavelengths from 520 nm to 1650 nm, available in various packages to suit different applications. These semiconductor laser diodes are designed with precision to provide coherent laser light, making them ideal for optoelectronic applications such as microwave photonics, OTDR, fiber-optic perimeter intrusion detection systems, optical communication, spectroscopy, sensing, and other photonic systems.
