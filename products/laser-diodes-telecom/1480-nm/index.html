Laser diodes Telecom

1480 nm fiber-coupled laser diodes

2 fiber-coupled laser diodes at 1480 nm with DFB chips, 50 mW, modulation up to 1 Gbps. Packages COAX, COAXB, COAXH, TH and BTF, with SMF and PMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

1480 nm is in the S-band of single-mode fiber and is one of the two pump bands of erbium-doped fiber, next to 980 nm.

Typical applications of our 1480 nm modules include laboratory pumping of erbium-doped fiber in EDFA experiments; testing and alignment of 1480 nm components such as 1480/1550 nm WDM couplers and EDFA parts; fiber sensing systems; spectroscopy; telecom and datacom links up to 1 Gbps; and research and education.

Our 1480 nm modules use DFB laser chips with a narrow linewidth. Parts of the coaxTEC series have a built-in TEC and thermistor, so the wavelength can be held stable over temperature. Tell us about your application, and we will help you choose the power, fiber and package.
