LD4B-1556-DFB-2.5G-3-ISO 1556 nm fiber-coupled DFB laser diode with isolator, 3 mW

Laser diode with a built-in isolator coupled to an optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Package
Fiber

Request a quote Datasheet PDF (567 KB)

Main features

  • Wavelength: 1555-1558 nm
  • Cavity type: DFB
  • Optical power in CW mode in single-mode fiber: >3 mW at Ith + 20mA, T = 25 °C
  • Data rate 2.5 Gbps
  • Package types: coaxial, coaxial with bracket
  • Built-in monitor photodiode
