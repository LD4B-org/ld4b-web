Laser diode with an integrated TEC and thermistor coupled to an optical fiber and packaged into a hermetic case.

Main features

  • Wavelength: 1310 nm
  • Cavity type: DFB
  • Linewidth: typ. 250 kHz, max 1000 kHz
  • Optical power in CW mode in single-mode fiber: 50 mW
  • Package type: coaxial with bracket
  • Built-in monitor photodiode
  • Built-in TEC and thermistor
