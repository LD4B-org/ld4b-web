Laser diodes VIS–NIR

905 nm fiber-coupled laser diodes

2 fiber-coupled laser diodes at 905 nm with Fabry-Perot chips, 3 to 80 mW. Packages COAX, COAXB, COAXH, TH and BTF, with SMF and PMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

905 nm is in the near infrared, invisible to the eye, where silicon photodiodes are still sensitive.

Typical applications of our 905 nm modules include development and testing of LiDAR and range finders; testing and calibration of 905 nm receivers such as silicon APDs; absorption spectroscopy; sensing systems with silicon photodetectors; near-infrared illumination delivered through fiber for imaging; and research and education.

Our 905 nm modules use Fabry-Perot laser chips coupled to optical fiber. Testing in pulse mode (test code P) can be ordered. Tell us about your application, and we will help you choose the power, fiber and package.
