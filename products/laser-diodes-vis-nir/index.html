Laser diodes VIS–NIR

We offer fiber-coupled laser diodes from 500 nm to 1064 nm, also known as pigtailed laser diodes: Fabry-Perot lasers for green, red and near-infrared wavelengths, and an 850 nm VCSEL for 10 Gbps links. They are coupled to single-mode (SMF), polarization-maintaining (PMF) or multimode (MMF) fibers and come in coaxial and butterfly packages, for spectroscopy, sensing, metrology and other photonic systems. For wavelengths from 1270 nm to 1654 nm, see our telecom laser diodes.

FAQ

Questions about VIS–NIR laser diodes

When should I choose polarization-maintaining fiber instead of standard single-mode fiber?

Choose polarization-maintaining (PM) fiber when your system is sensitive to polarization, for example in interferometric or coherent sensing, with external modulators, or with other polarization-dependent components, because in standard single-mode (SM) fiber the polarization drifts with bending, vibration and temperature. If polarization doesn't matter, standard SM fiber is the simpler choice. Most of our single-mode lasers and SLDs are available with either fiber, but note that coupling efficiency into PM fiber is typically lower.
