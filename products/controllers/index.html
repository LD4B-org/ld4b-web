Compact Laser Module With Manual Controller

Compact manual laser diode controller with an integrated fiber-coupled laser diode. Designed for laboratory, prototyping, and field applications where simplicity, portability, and safety are critical.

  • Can be equipped with any LD4B fiber-coupled laser diode 500 - 1650 nm
  • Constant current operation, simple manual control
  • USB Type-C power input, operates from any 5 V / 1 A USB power source (e.g. charger, power bank)
  • Soft-Start Current Ramp
  • Integrated cooling with a fan, does not require additional heat sink
  • Built-in safety protections (overcurrent protection, overtemperature protection, ESD protection, prevents enabling without current limiter)
