SLDs and LEDs

1550 nm fiber-coupled SLDs

One fiber-coupled superluminescent diode (SLD) at 1550 nm, 1 mW. Packages COAX, COAXB, TH and BTF, with SMF and PMF fiber. Built to order in Warsaw, from one piece.

Superluminescent diodes emit broadband, low-coherence light with the brightness of a laser in single-mode fiber. Their short coherence length avoids speckle and unwanted interference. Our 1550 nm SLD has a spectral width of typically 40 nm (FWHM), which covers the C-band.

Typical applications of our 1550 nm SLD include fiber sensing systems, including interrogation of fiber Bragg grating (FBG) sensors; optical coherence tomography (OCT); use as a broadband source for testing C-band components such as filters, couplers and WDMs; white-light interferometry for thickness and surface profile measurements; ellipsometry; and research and education.
