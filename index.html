LD coupling stability

Coupling efficiency that depends less on temperature

For a module without active thermal stabilization, the figure that matters is the front-to-rear tracking error: how much the power in the fiber varies with temperature while the monitor photodiode current is held constant.

Our optimized pigtailing keeps the tracking error at a typical 0.3 dB (±7 %) across the −40 to +85 °C temperature range for telecom wavelengths, while standard modules can show a tracking error of up to 1 dB (±20 %).

PD coupling technologies

Three coupling technologies for different requirements

A photodiode module is a compromise between responsivity, back-reflection and the fiber it has to accept. We offer three coupling technologies, each built for one of these priorities.

RM gives higher responsivity and low polarization-dependent loss, R50 keeps back-reflection low so the module does not disturb the rest of the fiber link, and LS couples multimode fiber into photodiodes with a smaller active area.

FAQ

Common questions

What is LD4B?

LD4B is a photonics company specializing in fiber-coupling of semiconductor laser diodes, superluminescent diodes (SLDs), and photodiodes. We design and manufacture advanced fiber-coupled laser and detector modules that deliver high performance and reliability.

Is LD4B a manufacturer?

Yes. LD4B manufactures fiber-coupled modules, but we do not grow wafers or make semiconductor chips. We source laser diodes, SLDs and photodiodes in TO-cans from chip manufacturers and specialize in what happens next: fiber coupling, active alignment, packaging and testing, carried out at our facility in Warsaw, Poland.

How can I place an order with LD4B?

To place an order, please send your request via email or use the contact form on our website. We will respond promptly, typically within one business day, to discuss your specifications and offer the best solution tailored to your needs. We welcome open communication to understand your requirements and help find the optimal fiber-coupled module for your application.

Does LD4B keep ready modules in stock?

Due to the wide variety of module configurations, including different package types, fiber types and lengths, and connector options, LD4B does not maintain ready-made modules in stock. Instead, we assemble each module promptly after receiving an order. Typical lead times for small orders are 2–3 weeks for coaxial modules and 3–4 weeks for 14-pin butterfly (BTF) modules. This approach ensures customized solutions tailored to each customer’s exact specifications.
