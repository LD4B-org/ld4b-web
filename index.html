LD coupling stability

Coupling efficiency that less depends on temperature

For a module without active thermal stabilization, the figure that matters is the front-to-rear tracking error: how much the power in the fiber varies with temperature while the monitor photodiode current is held constant.

Our optimized pigtailing keeps the tracking error at a typical 0.3 dB (±7 %) across the −40 to +85 °C temperature range, while commercial modules can show a tracking error of up to 1 dB (±20 %).

Why LD4B

Alignment and packaging is all we do

Thermally stable coupling

Typical front-to-rear tracking error of 0.3 dB (±7 %) at telecom wavelengths over −40…+85 °C with our stability-optimised pigtailing, against 0.8–1.0 dB common in commercial modules.

Any package, fiber and connector

Coaxial, DIL, DILRAD and butterfly packages; single-mode, polarization-maintaining or multimode fiber; FC/UPC, FC/APC and other connectors.

From one piece

No minimum order quantity. Each module is assembled after the order, so it is configured exactly as you need.

Short lead times

About 2–3 weeks for coaxial modules and 3–4 weeks for DIL and butterfly modules on small orders.

Tested for reliability

Module designs are tested following the Telcordia GR-468 standard: mechanical shock and vibration, thermal cycling, low- and high-temperature storage, fiber integrity and gross leak tests.

Made in the EU

Assembled in Warsaw, Poland, in a clean environment with ESD protection measures; RoHS and REACH compliant.

Applications

Where our modules work

OTDR and distributed sensing

Fabry-Perot and DFB laser diodes with sensitive PIN and avalanche photodiodes.

Microwave photonics

PIN photodiodes with 10 and 20 GHz bandwidth for tens of mW input, and DFB lasers up to 10 GHz.

Gas sensing

DFB lasers with TEC and thermistor that keep the wavelength on the gas absorption line.

OCT and fiber sensors

Superluminescent diodes at 830, 980, 1310 and 1550 nm.

Free-space optical (FSO) communication

2.5 and 10 Gbps APD-TIA and PIN-TIA receivers with multimode fiber for telescope coupling.

Laboratory and research

Modules for the Thorlabs LDM9LP mount and a compact laser module powered from USB-C.

FAQ

Common questions

What is LD4B?

LD4B is a photonics company specializing in fiber-coupling of semiconductor laser diodes, superluminescent diodes (SLDs), and photodiodes. We design and manufacture advanced fiber-coupled laser and detector modules that deliver high performance and reliability.

Is LD4B a manufacturer?

Yes, LD4B is a manufacturer specializing in alignment, and packaging; however, we do not produce wafers or grow semiconductor chips.

How can I place an order with LD4B?

To place an order, please send your request via email or use the contact form on our website. We will respond promptly—typically within one business day—to discuss your specifications and offer the best solution tailored to your needs. We welcome open communication to understand your requirements and help find the optimal fiber-coupled module for your application.

Does LD4B keep ready modules in stock?

Due to the wide variety of module configurations—including different package types, fiber types and lengths, and connector options—LD4B does not maintain ready-made modules in stock. Instead, we assemble each module promptly after receiving an order. Typical lead times for small orders are approximately 2 weeks for coaxial modules and 3 weeks for DIL-14-pin or BTF-14-pin modules. This approach ensures customized solutions tailored to each customer’s exact specifications.
